# Cloth Analytics Platform
## Business Context Document

### Company Overview

Cloth is an online streetwear brand specializing in trendy, high-quality apparel and accessories. Founded with a focus on bold aesthetics and urban culture, the brand has quickly gained traction among fashion-forward consumers aged 18-35. Cloth sells exclusively through its e-commerce platform, with a growing social media presence driving brand awareness and customer acquisition.

### Strategic Context

Cloth's accessories line currently underperforms relative to industry benchmarks—representing roughly 12% of revenue compared to the 25-30% typical of comparable streetwear brands. Leadership believes the category has significant untapped potential and is planning a Q2 re-brand of the accessories line to reposition it as a core part of the Cloth identity rather than a secondary offering.

To inform the re-brand strategy, leadership requires a deeper understanding of who is currently buying accessories, how those customers differ from apparel-only buyers, and what pricing and marketing approaches are most likely to drive growth. Customer and sales data exists but has not been consolidated into a unified view that supports strategic decision-making.

### Business Questions to Answer

- Who are our customers demographically, and how do different segments contribute to revenue?
- Where are our customers geographically concentrated, and are there underserved regions worth targeting?
- Which customer segments are already purchasing accessories, and what characterizes them?
- What are the seasonal patterns in order volume, and how should this inform re-brand timing?
- What is our average order value trend, and what does this suggest about accessories price point tolerance?
- How do accessories buyers differ from apparel-only customers in terms of loyalty and lifetime value?

### Stakeholders and Their Needs

| Stakeholder | Role | What They Need |
|-------------|------|----------------|
| Marketing Director | Owns campaign strategy and customer acquisition | Customer segments by demographics, geographic concentration, and purchasing frequency to decide targeting strategy for the accessories re-brand campaign |
| Chief Operating Officer | Owns operations, fulfillment, and supply chain | Order patterns, seasonal trends, average order value, and fulfillment metrics to plan inventory levels and logistics capacity for increased accessories volume |
| Chief Executive Officer | Strategic oversight and investor relations | High-level dashboard showing customer growth trends, revenue by category, and accessories performance indicators to communicate progress to stakeholders |

### Success Criteria

**Marketing Effectiveness**
- Marketing Director can identify the top 3 customer segments by revenue contribution without requesting custom analysis
- Geographic distribution of customers is clearly visualized to inform regional campaign budget allocation
- Accessories buyer profiles are quantified to identify target audiences for the re-brand campaign

**Operational Readiness**
- COO has visibility into seasonal order volume patterns with sufficient granularity to forecast accessories demand
- Average order value trends are tracked over time to validate accessories pricing strategy decisions
- Fulfillment patterns (order size distribution, shipping destination breakdown) are available to plan logistics capacity

**Solution Quality**
- All three stakeholders can self-serve answers to their core questions through interactive dashboards
- Data refreshes daily to ensure decisions reflect recent customer behavior
- Architecture supports tracking accessories re-brand performance without requiring platform redesign
- Total infrastructure cost remains under $50/month at current data volumes
